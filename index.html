Travel Diary

Exploring different places,immersing myself in the serenity of nature, spending time with diverse communities, and celebrating their festivals—these experiences are truly unmatched. Personally, I prefer mountains over beaches, as they offer a unique sense of peace and adventure. Here are a few shots from some of my trips.

Manali

Koksar is a small picturesque village located in the Lahaul and Spiti district of Himachal Pradesh, India. Nestled in the beautiful Himalayan region, Koksar serves as a gateway to the famous Pin Valley National Park and the scenic Atal Tunnel.

Agra

The Taj Mahal is an ivory-white marble mausoleum on the right bank of the river Yamuna in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. It was commissioned in 1631 by the fifth Mughal emperor, Shah Jahan to house the tomb of his beloved wife, Mumtaz Mahal; it also houses the tomb of Shah Jahan himself

Dhanushkodi

It is one of the Tirthas in the island of Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, India. This is located at the easternmost tip of Rameswaram Island.This bridge was used for his army to cross over to Lanka for the victorious war against Ravana.

Kailasagiri

Kailasagiri,located in Chintamani taluk of Kolar district, are about 65 km from Bangalore. Bheema Bakana betta is a hillock in the region where, if legend is to be believed, Bheema killed Bakasura.

Minakshi-temple

It is a historic Hindu temple located on the southern bank of the Vaigai River in the temple city of Madurai,Tamil Nadu,India. It is dedicated to the goddess Meenakshi Amman(form of Parvati) and her consort Shiva.

Shohosrodhara

Sohosrodhara Waterfall is located in the Sitakunda Ecopark in the forest area of the traditional Chandranath Rijav Forest of Chittagong District. You can visit during the rainy season, you can see the fountain full of water and enjoy the beauty of the fountain to the fullest.
